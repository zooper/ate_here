Ate Here Support

Ate Here is a private restaurant journal for remembering places you visited.

Need help?

Email tomas@jonsson.io and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a description of what happened. Please do not send private photos or precise location details unless they are necessary to explain the issue.

Photos are not appearing

Open Settings on your iPhone, choose Ate Here, choose Photos, and grant access to the photos you want to use. Limited Photos access is supported.

Automatic matching

Automatic matching is optional. It runs when enabled and may also receive occasional background time from iOS. Suggestions always wait for your confirmation.

iCloud backup

iCloud backup is optional and uses your private iCloud database. It stores journal metadata and PhotoKit references, not original photo files.
