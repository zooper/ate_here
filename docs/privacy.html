Ate Here Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Ate Here is a private restaurant journal. Your journal stays on your device unless you explicitly enable the optional iCloud backup.

Photos and location

Ate Here accesses only the photos you select or allow through Photos permissions. It reads photo dates, locations, asset identifiers, and small temporary previews to identify possible restaurant visits. Photo analysis happens on your device. Ate Here does not upload your photos or use them to train models.

Location data is used to match photos with nearby Apple Maps places and, if you choose, to define a private home area that automatic scans ignore.

Apple Maps

Ate Here uses Apple Maps and MapKit to search for and display restaurant information. It stores Apple Maps place identifiers with your journal entries, but does not operate or maintain a restaurant directory.

Optional iCloud backup

If you enable iCloud backup, Ate Here stores journal metadata in your private iCloud database, including dates, notes, tags, ratings, coordinates, Apple Maps place identifiers, and PhotoKit asset references. Original photos are not uploaded. Apple’s privacy terms apply to iCloud.

Sharing and tracking

Ate Here has no developer-operated backend, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, account system, or hosted AI service. We do not sell your data.

Deletion

Delete visits and their app-owned metadata from within Ate Here. You can also disable or remove the optional iCloud backup through the app and your device’s iCloud settings.
